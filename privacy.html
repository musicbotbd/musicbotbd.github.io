Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This page explains what Music Bot ("the bot") keeps when you use it in Discord. The short version: it keeps only what it needs to play music, and it never sells or shares your data.

What the bot stores

  • Server settings: your server's ID, whether autoplay is on, and the request channel, if an admin set one. Kept until you change them or remove the bot.
  • The current queue: song titles and links, which voice and text channel it is playing in, and recently played song IDs (so autoplay doesn't repeat songs). This lets the bot carry on after a restart. It is replaced as the queue changes and cleared by /stop.
  • Technical logs: errors and events such as which song failed to load. Logs are rotated and old ones are deleted automatically.

Messages

The bot reads message text only in a request channel that a server admin turned on with /requestchannel, and only to find the song you typed. It does not store those messages. In every other channel it ignores messages.

What the bot does not collect

  • No passwords, emails, phone numbers or payment details.
  • No voice: the bot joins voice channels deafened and never records anything.
  • No tracking or advertising.

Other services

To find and play songs the bot contacts YouTube, and for Spotify, Apple Music or SoundCloud links it reads the public page of that link. Only the song name or link is sent, never information about you. The bot runs on a cloud server in Europe. Discord's own Privacy Policy covers everything Discord itself handles.

Donations

If you donate by bKash or Nagad, the payment happens inside those apps. The bot and this website never see your account details.

Removing your data

Use /stop to clear the queue, and turn settings off with /autoplay off or /requestchannel off. Removing the bot from your server stops all use of your server's data. To ask for anything else to be deleted, contact the developer through GitHub.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
