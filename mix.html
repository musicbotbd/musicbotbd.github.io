Music Bot Add to Discord

Play your own YouTube Mix

YouTube makes every Mix just for the person watching it, and shuffles it again every time it opens. So when you give the bot a Mix link, it gets a general Mix, not yours. This button copies the exact songs you see, in the same order.

Only the video ids are copied. Your YouTube login and cookies never leave your browser.

🎵 Copy Mix for Music Bot

Don't click it here: drag it to your bookmarks bar.

How to set it up (once)

  1. Show your bookmarks bar. Chrome, Edge, Opera: Ctrl + Shift + B.
  2. Drag the purple button above onto the bookmarks bar.

How to use it

  1. On YouTube, open a Mix or a playlist. The song list shows on the right.
  2. Click the bookmark. It copies a link (the list starts at the song playing now, up to 50 songs).
  3. In Discord, type /stop if something is already playing.
  4. Type /play, paste the link, and press Enter.

The bot plays your list in order. If it was a Mix, it keeps going with similar songs after that.

Works in desktop browsers. Phones don't support bookmark buttons like this.
