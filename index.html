Commands

All commands are slash commands. Type / in Discord to see them. /help shows this list in Discord.

/playSong name, or a YouTube / Spotify / Apple Music / SoundCloud song, album or playlist link
/skipSkip to the next song
/pausePause the music
/resumeContinue after a pause
/seek 1:30Jump to a time in the current song
/loopRepeat the song, the whole queue, or turn it off
/queueSee what's playing and what's next
/shuffleMix up the order of the queue
/remove 3Remove a song by its number in /queue
/volume 50Set the volume from 0 to 100
/autoplayKeep playing similar songs when the queue ends
/requestchannelAdmins: a channel where typing a song plays it
/stopStop, clear the queue and leave
/helpShow every command in Discord

Want your own personal YouTube Mix in the exact order you see it? Use the Copy Mix button.

Questions

Is Music Bot really free?

Yes. There is no premium version. Every command works for everyone.

How do I add Music Bot to my Discord server?

Click Add to Discord, pick your server, join a voice channel and type /play with a song name or link.

Why does a song take a few seconds to start?

Each song is loaded right before it plays, one at a time. That keeps the bot light so it can stay free and online.

My YouTube Mix plays different songs than on YouTube. Why?

YouTube makes every Mix personally for the person watching, and reshuffles it each time. The bot can't see your account, so it gets a general Mix. To play exactly the list you see, use the Copy Mix button.

Can Music Bot play Spotify and Apple Music playlists?

Yes. Paste a Spotify or Apple Music song, album or playlist link into /play. The bot reads the song names and plays each song from YouTube. Now and then it may pick a different version (live, cover).

The bot isn't responding. What can I do?

Make sure you're in a voice channel and the bot can see and join it. If it still doesn't answer, wait a minute: it restarts itself after problems.
